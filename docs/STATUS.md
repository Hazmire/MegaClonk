continued ten-minute stages, saved events, two main bosses with three specials each,
two timed minibosses, endless ghosts, supplied forest assets and planar terrain,
revised abilities/movement upgrades, per-weapon attributes and DPS, silver progression,
real quests, tiered one-purchase shops, HUD/build-screen improvements and red hit flashes.
