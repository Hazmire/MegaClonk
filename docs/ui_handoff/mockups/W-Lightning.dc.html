Lightning Staff

LOOK

  • Blue bolts drop from the sky straight onto enemies' heads — a dark-blue outer stroke around a near-white core.
  • Struck enemies flash pure white for a few frames, with a pale ring at the head and a brief blue tint over the whole screen.
  • Bounces arc sideways from the struck enemy to the next one.

BEHAVIOUR

  • No travel time: the strike lands on the target instantly.
  • Size widens the strike so one bolt can hit a whole cluster.
