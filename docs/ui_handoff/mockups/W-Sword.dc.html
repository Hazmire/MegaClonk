Sword

LOOK

  • A hard-edged white crescent with a black rim and a thin pale-blue inner edge, swept in front of the player.
  • It animates on stepped frames rather than smoothly, for a crunchy PSX feel.
  • White and pale-blue speed streaks fly off the arc; each slash alternates direction and the last is the biggest.
  • Struck enemies flash white and get pushed back further with each hit.

BEHAVIOUR

  • Slashes toward the closest enemy.
  • Projectile count turns one slash into a burst (×3 shown), followed by the cooldown.
