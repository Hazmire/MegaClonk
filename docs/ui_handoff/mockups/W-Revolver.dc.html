WEAPON · DRAFT

Revolver

LOOK

  • A small, bright silver bullet with a cool glow.
  • A trail of grey smoke puffs that spawn as it passes, then swell, rise and fade — the oldest puffs disappear first.
  • Star-shaped muzzle flash; a white spark burst where it hits or ricochets.

BEHAVIOUR

  • Fires at the nearest enemy and stops at the first hit.
  • Bounces ricochet the bullet to the next closest enemy, still trailing smoke.
