# Dark combat feedback and health HUD12

Enemy hit/death audio is disabled. Killing enemies, self-destruction and death-triggered blasts add no enemy sound. Enemy audio IDs and legacy death calls are rejected, while genuine weapon firing/impacts remain audible. Player damage, movement, rewards, ambience, music and existing boss attack warnings retain their own roles. Unused enemy-death recordings and old explosion paths are excluded from exports.

The supplied Pixel Combat set was re-curated for heavier flame bursts, electrical impacts, hollow magic and metallic pickups. All106 selected takes have a restrained5kHz low-pass, soft saturation/10bit grain and lower per-event playback pitch, with source identity/variation preserved. The original supplied music remains. Source offsets/processed hashes: docs/audio/CURATION.json; CC BY4.0 attribution and modifications remain included. This is a directed dark/crunchy sound pass based on source categories and waveform processing, **not an auditioned judgement of the final mix**.

Equipped tomes occupy their own horizontal row immediately under weapons. Traits/ultimates follow, with spacing reserved for the health display. The left health bar includes current/maximum HP centered inside it. Both health bars use dark red#801421 with a dark maroon background. The64×7px character bar follows the projected position beneath the player's feet; it hides outside gameplay and when the player is behind the camera. It is visual only and does not alter movement, collision or health rules.

Focused tests cover silent hit/death/blast feedback, preserved weapon mappings, separate inventory rows, matching HP values/colors, character-bar tracking and visibility. The audio and previous v9 UI suites also pass. Screenshot: [HUD](images/dark-feedback/hud.png). Final CI/export/download verification is recorded in releases/DARK_FEEDBACK_VALIDATION.json after publication. Native Windows/audio-device listening and existing dense-horde performance remain playtest limits.
