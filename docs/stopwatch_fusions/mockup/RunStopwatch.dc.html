The Stopwatch

TIME STOPS AROUND YOU · THE SCREEN IS THE CLOCK

The "STOPWATCH 2.9s" bar is gone. Picking it up sends a wave of frozen time out from the player: the world turns cold and grey and the rain stops in mid-air, while you stay in colour. The time left shows two ways: a brass pocket watch under the run timer, and 60 ticks around the edge of the screen that go out one by one. Then colour floods back in.

WORKS FOR ANY DURATION The ticks and the watch show the fraction left, not seconds. All 60 ticks are always one whole Stopwatch, however long it lasts. With items that make it 5 s, 8 s or 20 s, the ticks just go out more slowly. Nothing is ever cut off or overflows. The number in the watch face shows the exact seconds left (one decimal place under 10 s, whole seconds above), so a long freeze still reads precisely. Extending mid-freeze (a second pickup, or an item that adds time on kill): the total becomes "time left + added", the ticks wind back up to the new fraction in 0.3 s, and the tag shows how much was added. The freeze wave doesn't replay, because time is already stopped. The red warning is always the last 1.0 s, whatever the total, so the cue means the same thing every time. The Hourglass (5 s freeze on a lethal hit) can use the same look and ticks, since it already runs on the same hostile-time freeze.
READABLE IN A SWARMThe tint and the edge ticks read in peripheral vision, so the player never has to look away from the fight. The HUD stays in full colour, and so does the player.
FROZEN ENEMIESEnemies, ghosts and projectiles stop where they are and take the cold tint with a faint ice rim. On release they jolt for a frame before moving, so the restart feels physical.
SOUNDThe existing stopwatch pickup sound plays on the wave. A soft tick could play every second while it runs, and the last second could tick faster to match the red. That would need new sounds; it's optional.
